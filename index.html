Getting drunk on education

Add your crew, draw a card, answer the question. The card says who's up and how many sips are on the line.

Players
Add at least 2 players. Each person gets a suit.
Sips
Ace = 1 sip, 2–9 = face value, 10/J/Q/K = 10. Chill halves it, rounded up.
Difficulty
Questions are a mix of easy, medium and hard.
Shot clock
How it works. Every player gets a stack of 13 cards (Ace through King) in their suit, and the whole deck is shuffled together. Draw a card: the suit picks who's up, and the rank sets the sips (Ace = 1, 2–9 = face value, 10/J/Q/K = 10). Answer right and you give out that many sips. Get it wrong and you drink them.

Difficulty. In Extra trashed, A–4 get easy questions, 5–9 medium, and 10–K hard, so the big sips come with the hard questions. Lightly trashed flips it: low cards get the hard questions and 10–K get easy ones.
